MICA: A Multimodal AI-Based Clinical Decision Support and Automated Medical Reporting System

MICA is an AI-powered clinical support platform designed to improve preliminary clinical assessment, medical information integration, and clinical documentation through multimodal data processing.

The system integrates Natural Language Processing (NLP), Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), speaker diarization, Medical Computer Vision, and Image Processing to analyze heterogeneous clinical data, including patient-reported symptoms, medical conversation recordings, radiological images, and laboratory test results.

The platform supports two primary workflows:

1. Patient-Oriented Clinical Assessment:
Patients can provide their symptoms through text or voice and upload relevant medical images and laboratory reports. The system processes these inputs to generate a structured summary of the reported symptoms, extracted medical information, and relevant findings from the submitted documents and images. Based on validated clinical guidelines and appropriate safety constraints, it can provide preliminary, non-diagnostic guidance, identify potential warning signs, and recommend an appropriate level of medical evaluation. A concise, structured clinical summary can then be provided for review by a qualified healthcare professional.

2. Physician-Oriented Automated Documentation:
The system analyzes recorded clinical encounters using speech recognition and speaker diarization to distinguish between physician and patient speech. It extracts relevant clinical information, integrates it with available radiological and laboratory findings, and generates a structured medical report in Arabic. The report can include the presenting complaint, history of present illness, relevant medical history, clinical findings, investigation results, assessment, and documented plan, subject to physician review and correction.

MICA aims to reduce administrative workload, improve clinical information organization, facilitate communication between patients and healthcare professionals, and support more efficient clinical workflows while maintaining human oversight, patient privacy, and clinical safety.
